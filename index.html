How this estimate works ＋

Each selected service is charged per sample. Add separate groups for different analyses, targets, or preparation needs. Uncheck services you do not require.

The paired ¹⁰Be + ²⁶Al option adds the sheet’s ²⁶Al chemistry, AMS, and ICP-OES rates to the ¹⁰Be workflow. The sheet describes ²⁶Al chemistry as an additional charge when both are analyzed; standalone ²⁶Al pricing needs confirmation.

Gas and solid AMS targets are alternatives for conventional ¹⁴C. Proposed bulk discount: 10% off non-AMS fees for samples above the threshold in each group. GI faculty as lead PI: no further bulk discount. Other ETH lead-PI: threshold of 10 for in-situ ¹⁴C and 50 for conventional ¹⁴C; no bulk discount for other analyses. PI outside ETH: threshold of 20 for all analyses, reduced to 10 for in-situ ¹⁴C. Only samples above the threshold receive the discount; earlier samples remain at the full rate. Discounts do not stack. Keep each batch of samples requiring the same analysis and services in one group. This policy is a proposal added to the calculator; the source fee sheet does not specify a discount percentage. For grant budgeting, the sheet recommends rates for collaborations with a PI outside ETH, including AMS even where fees are covered.
